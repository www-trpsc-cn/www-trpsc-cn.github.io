# www-trpsc-cn.github.io
  中国人文复兴会(The Renaissance Party Of Soviet China)，简称TRPSC。是一个致力于复兴社会科学与人文主义的组织和党派，秉持“国家要复兴，人文必振兴”的理念，为人民大众服务，并为文，史，哲，法，政，地等传统社会科学复兴贡献绵薄之力。
 The Renaissance Party Of Soviet China, abbreviated as TRPSC. It is an organization and party committed to the revival of social science and humanism. Adhering to the concept of 'the country needs to be revived, the humanities must be revitalized', it serves the people and contributes to the revival of traditional social sciences such as literature, history, philosophy, law, politics and geography.
